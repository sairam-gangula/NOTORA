# Notora — Capture. Organise. Create.

**Notora** is an AI-powered personal productivity application designed to bring note-taking, task management, reminders, calendar management and intelligent AI assistance together in one seamless experience.

More than a traditional notes application, Notora aims to be a personal productivity workspace that helps users capture their thoughts, organise information, manage their time and turn ideas into actionable tasks.

The goal is to simplify everyday productivity by combining essential tools with intelligent AI capabilities in a beautifully designed, intuitive and distraction-free mobile application.

---

## ✨ Product Vision

Notora aims to bridge the gap between capturing information and taking action.

People constantly generate ideas, make plans, remember deadlines and manage responsibilities. However, these activities are often scattered across multiple applications.

Notora brings these experiences together in one place.

Whether users want to quickly write down an idea, organise their notes, create a task, schedule a reminder or use AI to structure their thoughts, Notora provides a unified environment to manage it all.

**The core philosophy is simple:**

- Capture anything, anytime.
- Organise information effortlessly.
- Let AI simplify repetitive work.
- Turn thoughts into actionable tasks.
- Stay on top of everyday responsibilities.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Notora follows a premium, minimalist and iOS-inspired design language.

The interface is designed around simplicity, readability, consistency and intuitive interactions.

**Brand Identity: Sand & Terracotta**

| Colour | Hex Code | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Sand | `#F4EFE7` | Primary background |
| Beige | `#E8D9C6` | Secondary surfaces |
| Terracotta | `#C77E61` | Primary accent |
| Charcoal | `#292824` | Primary text |
| Muted Stone | `#A3A18D` | Secondary accents |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and surfaces |
| Dark Terracotta | `#A85F40` | Pressed states |
| Warm Peach | `#F4E4D6` | AI highlights |

**Typography**
- Manrope for headings.
- SF Pro Text for iOS body typography.
- Inter as a cross-platform fallback.

**Design Principles**
- Minimalist interfaces.
- Generous whitespace.
- Rounded corners and subtle shadows.
- Smooth transitions and micro-animations.
- Consistent iconography.
- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Accessible typography and touch targets.
- Dedicated light and dark themes.

AI functionality should feel naturally integrated into the experience rather than like a separate chatbot attached to a notes application.

## 🧭 Application Navigation

The proposed primary navigation consists of five sections:

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home | Dashboard, recent notes, pinned notes and quick capture |
| AI | Conversational assistant and AI-powered tools |
| Tasks | Today's tasks, upcoming tasks and completed tasks |
| Calendar | Events, reminders and schedules |
| More | Folders, tags, settings and personalisation |

A prominent quick-create action will remain easily accessible throughout the application.

## 🛠️ Development Scope

Notora is being approached with an MVP-first development strategy.

The initial release prioritises the core productivity experience rather than attempting to implement every long-term feature immediately.

**MVP Priorities**

1. Home dashboard and navigation.

3. Rich-text editing and automatic saving.
4. Folders and tags.
5. Integrated AI writing assistance.
6. Smart capture and task extraction.
